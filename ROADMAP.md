PushTakim — Long-Term Roadmap

This document preserves ideas and directions that should survive beyond individual events.
Items here are NOT automatically approved implementation requirements.

Core Vision

Transform PushTakim from an event-focused website into a useful long-term platform for the movement community.

⸻

1 — Events

Continue supporting community events while making event administration simpler.

Potential improvements:

* Event pages
* Registration
* Ticketing
* Schedules
* Challenges/competitions
* Participant management
* Health declarations
* Waivers
* Results
* Recaps/media

Events should eventually become one part of the platform rather than the entire platform.

⸻

2 — PushTakim Magazine

Build a content/knowledge section.

Possible content:

* Training
* Parkour
* Movement
* Rehabilitation
* Injury prevention
* Athlete development
* Research explanations
* Interviews
* Community stories
* Tutorials
* Opinion/perspective pieces
* Event recaps

The content should preserve Ken’s combination of practical athletic experience and professional movement/rehabilitation knowledge.

⸻

3 — Spot Map

Develop a map of useful parkour/movement locations.

Potential future concepts:

* Interactive map
* Search by area
* Spot pages
* Photos
* Training possibilities/features
* Community submissions
* Updates
* Useful notes

Do NOT implement a database architecture until requirements are defined.

⸻

4 — Research

Develop a permanent research/education area.

Current/previous research activity includes work around characterization and understanding of sports injuries, with collaboration involving healthcare/research organizations.

Possible future functionality:

* Research information
* Participation links
* Accessible explanations
* Findings/results
* Educational content derived from research
* Resources for athletes/coaches

Research should be presented responsibly and should clearly distinguish evidence, professional interpretation and general training content.

⸻

5 — Movement Knowledge

Create useful resources that connect:

Training ↔ performance ↔ injury prevention ↔ rehabilitation ↔ return to sport

The site should eventually help athletes understand their bodies and training rather than merely advertise services.

⸻

6 — Community Directory

Potential future representation for:

* Communities
* Training groups
* Halls
* Coaches
* Athletes
* Organizations
* Movement disciplines

Exact functionality to be defined later.

⸻

7 — Partners & Supporters

Create a sustainable way to recognize collaborators.

Could include:

* Partner organizations
* Event supporters
* Brands
* Venues
* Community contributors
* Researchers/professionals
* Volunteers

⸻

8 — Acknowledgements / Thank You

Create a dedicated way of preserving credit for people who helped PushTakim projects happen.

The platform should not lose the human history behind the project as it grows.

⸻

9 — Athlete / Coach Tools

Future exploration may include useful tools for athletes/coaches.

No specific implementation is currently assumed.

Potential directions can be evaluated based on actual community needs rather than building features simply because they are technically possible.

⸻

10 — Archive / History

Preserve meaningful parts of PushTakim history:

* Events
* Competitions
* Photos/videos
* Results
* Collaborations
* Milestones

Past events should not necessarily disappear simply because ticket sales have ended.

Where appropriate, they can become archive/recap content.

⸻

Priority Principle

Do not attempt to build everything simultaneously.

After urgent event operations are completed:

1. Stabilize existing infrastructure.
2. Fix participant/forms/admin data flow.
3. Document the project.
4. Decide which long-term feature provides the most value.
5. Build incrementally.
