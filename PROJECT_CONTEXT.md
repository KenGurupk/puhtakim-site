PushTakim — Project Context

Persistent context for ChatGPT / Codex.
Read this document before making significant changes to the PushTakim project.
Do not treat PushTakim as only an event-ticketing website.

1. What is PushTakim?

PushTakim / פושטקים is a growing movement, community and platform centered around movement culture.

It connects athletes, coaches, practitioners, communities and different movement disciplines.

Parkour is a major part of its DNA, but the vision is broader than parkour alone.

PushTakim should encourage:

* Movement and physical exploration
* Parkour and freerunning
* Tricking and acrobatics
* Strength and body control
* Creativity and improvisation
* Cross-disciplinary learning
* Injury prevention and smarter training
* Rehabilitation and return to sport
* Community collaboration
* Knowledge sharing
* Research
* Exposure to new athletes, coaches, communities and disciplines

The goal is not simply to create events.

The long-term goal is to build infrastructure and a home for movement culture in Israel.

3. How PushTakim Should Feel

PushTakim must NOT feel like a generic corporate sports company.

Its personality is:

* Urban
* Playful
* Creative
* Slightly rebellious
* Community-driven
* Authentic
* Energetic
* Professional without becoming sterile
* Serious about knowledge without taking itself too seriously

There is room for:

* Street culture
* Humor
* Stickers
* Graffiti-inspired elements
* Challenges
* Inside jokes
* Community language
* Unexpected interactions

The project should retain the feeling of people who actually train and belong to the culture.

⸻

4. Communication Style

Communication should generally be:

Short → clear → confident → human.

Avoid unnecessarily corporate or sales-heavy language.

Do not oversell something when its value can speak for itself.

For community communication, the preferred feeling is:

“You should be part of this.”

rather than:

“We are trying to convince you to buy something.”

Hebrew communication can be informal and conversational.

English may be used where it fits the movement/sports culture naturally.

⸻

5. Design Philosophy

The visual identity should remain recognizable and energetic.

Important qualities:

* Strong contrast
* Urban/street influence
* Clean enough to understand quickly
* Personality without visual chaos
* Mobile-first usability
* Movement and interaction where they add value

Existing visual decisions should NOT be casually replaced by generic templates.

Before redesigning an existing component, inspect the current implementation and understand why it exists.

⸻

6. PushTakim Is Bigger Than The Current Tour

The 2026 PushTakim Tour is an important project, but it is NOT the final purpose of the website.

The website should gradually evolve into a broader movement platform.

Future development includes, among other ideas:

Magazine / Knowledge Platform

Articles, educational content and professional material relating to:

* Parkour
* Movement
* Training
* Rehabilitation
* Injury prevention
* Athlete development
* Community
* Research
* Interviews
* Experiences and stories

Spot Map

A community-oriented map of movement/parkour spots.

Potential future functionality may include:

* Locations
* Photos
* Spot information
* Training possibilities
* Community contributions
* Safety/useful notes

The exact implementation should be designed later rather than assumed now.

Research

PushTakim is involved in promoting research around sports injuries and movement.

Research-related work should eventually have a meaningful place on the platform rather than being treated as a temporary event campaign.

Community & Collaboration

The platform should highlight:

* Communities
* Coaches
* Athletes
* Partner organizations
* Halls
* Brands
* Contributors
* People helping the movement ecosystem grow

Acknowledgements / Credits

The people and organizations who help build projects should receive visible recognition.

PushTakim should remember the community behind the work.

7. Development Principle

When working on PushTakim:

Preserve before replacing.

Before making substantial changes:

1. Inspect the existing repository.
2. Understand the current implementation.
3. Check existing functionality.
4. Check previous project-context documentation.
5. Determine whether the requested change affects another feature.
6. Modify only what is necessary.

Do not rebuild working functionality simply because another implementation appears cleaner.

⸻

8. AI Collaboration Principle

ChatGPT and Codex are long-term collaborators on this project.

They should:

* Preserve previous decisions.
* Read project documentation before working.
* Avoid inventing missing requirements.
* Ask/check when uncertainty could cause destructive changes.
* Prefer inspecting existing implementation over guessing.
* Explain risky changes before performing them.
* Keep future development possibilities in mind without unnecessarily overengineering today’s task.

The objective is continuity:

New chat ≠ new project.

The repository and these context files represent the project’s persistent memory.
